PURVA SILVERSKY

Overview

Purva Silversky is a luxury residential development in Hebbagodi, Electronic City, offering thoughtfully designed 3 and 4 BHK apartments for those who value space, calm, and contemporary living. Envisioned by Hafeez Contractor and spread across 6.99 acres, the development is planned to complement the rhythm of modern life, allowing work and home to coexist with ease.

With just 356 exclusive apartments, Purva Silversky reflects the advantage of low-density living in Bengaluru’s technology corridor. Landscaped green spaces, wellness-inspired amenities, and elegant architectural detailing create an address that feels open, composed, and quietly restorative.

Here, balance is experienced through everyday living in unhurried moments, generous spaces, and time that feels naturally your own. Here, balance isn’t an idea. It’s a way of living.

A residential community in Electronic City where 88% open-to-sky living creates a rare sense of space and calm. Curated wellness zones, forest bathing trails, yoga decks, and restorative outdoor spaces reflect a low-density, wellness-oriented way of living.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Purva Silversky located?

Purva Silversky, a 3 and 4 BHK residential development in Electronic City, is located in Hebbagodi on Huskur Road, one of Bengaluru’s fastest-growing residential and employment corridors. These apartments in Hebbagodi enjoy close proximity to major IT and technology parks, along with well-established social infrastructure that supports everyday convenience.

With seamless access to Hosur Road and NICE Road, this residential project in Electronic City offers easy connectivity across the city while maintaining a balanced relationship between work, lifestyle, and mobility—making it a considered choice for contemporary urban living.

How many residences are available?

Purva Silversky comprises 356 exclusive residences spread across 7 acres, offering a curated selection of 3 BHK and 4 BHK apartments for sale. The community also features duplex residences and penthouses, reinforcing the character of low-density apartment living in Bengaluru.

These premium residences are designed to accommodate varied lifestyle preferences, with generous layouts that emphasise space, comfort, and a sense of understated elegance.

What lifestyle amenities are available in these luxury apartments in Hebbagodi?

Purva Silversky offers a curated collection of 45+ lifestyle amenities designed to support wellness, leisure, and everyday indulgence. At the heart of the community is an expansive 17,000 sq. ft. clubhouse, featuring a state-of-the-art gym, indoor sports facilities, spa, café, lounges, a wellness clinic, and dedicated yoga decks.

Beyond the clubhouse, thoughtfully designed outdoor experiences such as culinary gardens, art lofts, an amphitheatre, treehouse, and shaded canopy walks create spaces for relaxation, creativity, and connection—shaping a lifestyle that feels immersive, balanced, and quietly enriching.

What makes Purva Silversky’s upcoming apartments near Infosys Electronic City unique?

Purva Silversky stands apart with its thoughtfully planned low-density living, offering exceptional privacy and tranquillity. With 88% open spaces, these apartments for sale in Electronic City promotes healthier, greener, and more breathable surroundings.

Paired with iconic architecture by Hafeez Contractor, it delivers a holistic residential experience that blends aesthetics, comfort, and smart planning for an elevated lifestyle.

What are the typologies available?

Purva Silversky offers a curated selection of spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK apartments in Electronic City, Hebbagodi, thoughtfully planned to suit varied lifestyle preferences. The development also includes limited duplex residences and premium penthouses, designed for those seeking larger layouts and elevated living.

How is the connectivity for Purva Silversky, apartments in Electronic City?

Purva Silversky enjoys strong connectivity within Electronic City and South Bengaluru, ensuring effortless access to key destinations. Major IT and technology parks are located close by, enabling convenient and efficient daily commutes.

Reputed educational institutions, healthcare facilities, shopping destinations, and everyday conveniences are all within a 5–20 minute drive, offering a well-connected lifestyle that balances work, mobility, and daily living with ease.

Is Purva Silversky a good investment opportunity for apartments for sale in Electronic City?

Yes, Purva Silversky is located within one of Bengaluru’s most resilient and high-growth corridors, offering strong potential for long-term capital appreciation and sustained rental demand.

The location benefits from operational metro connectivity, ongoing and proposed government-led infrastructure investments, and the planned widening of Hosur Road, which will further enhance regional accessibility. The emergence of life sciences hubs, pharmaceutical zones, and industrial developments around Hosur, along with the continued expansion of global IT and commercial establishments in Electronic City, reinforces the area’s economic depth and future growth outlook.

What educational and healthcare facilities are near Purva Silversky in Electronic City?

Purva Silversky is surrounded by a strong network of reputed educational and healthcare institutions, making it well suited for families and long-term living. Well-known schools such as MS Dhoni Global School, along with colleges and professional institutes including Xavier Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship, are located within easy reach.

Residents also benefit from proximity to established healthcare facilities like Narayana Multi-Speciality Hospital, offering comprehensive medical care and emergency services. This well-developed social infrastructure adds everyday convenience while ensuring comfort and peace of mind.

Are there shopping and entertainment options near these 4 BHK luxury apartments in Hebbagodi?

Purva Silversky enjoys close proximity to a wide range of shopping, dining, and leisure destinations, offering residents everyday convenience as well as weekend indulgences. Popular retail and lifestyle hubs such as M5 Ecity Mall, DMart Electronic City, and DMart Bommasandra cater to daily needs and shopping essentials.

For recreation and leisure, destinations including Gold Coins Club & Resort, Nirantara Resort & Spa, and The Oterra Hotel offer curated dining, wellness, and social experiences.
