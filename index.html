PURVA SILVERSKY

Overview

Purva Silversky is a luxury residential development in Hebbagodi, Electronic City, offering thoughtfully designed 3 and 4 BHK apartments for those who value space, calm, and contemporary living. Envisioned by Hafeez Contractor and spread across 6.99 acres, the development is planned to complement the rhythm of modern life, allowing work and home to coexist with ease.

With just 356 exclusive apartments, Purva Silversky reflects the advantage of low-density living in Bengaluru’s technology corridor. Landscaped green spaces, wellness-inspired amenities, and elegant architectural detailing create an address that feels open, composed, and quietly restorative.

Here, balance is experienced through everyday living in unhurried moments, generous spaces, and time that feels naturally your own. Here, balance isn’t an idea. It’s a way of living.

A LIFE COMPOSED IN BALANCE

Key Features

A residential community in Electronic City where 88% open-to-sky living creates a rare sense of space and calm. Curated wellness zones, forest bathing trails, yoga decks, and restorative outdoor spaces reflect a low-density, wellness-oriented way of living.

A 17,700 Sq.Ft. clubhouse anchors these premium apartments in Electronic City, complemented by contemporary infrastructure and immersive experiences such as culinary gardens, art lofts, and shaded canopy walks shaping a gated community lifestyle that feels expansive and quietly refined.
